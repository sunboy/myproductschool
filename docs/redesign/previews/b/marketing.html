The method

Four moves, drilled until they're instinct.

Every case study, every mock interview, every autopsy routes back to the same four-move sequence. It's not a mnemonic. It's the actual shape of good product reasoning.

Frame

Find the problem behind the problem

Separate the symptom from the root cause before you touch a solution.

List

Widen the option space

Generate solutions that are structurally different, not five versions of the same idea.

Optimize

Name the tradeoff, not the preference

Pick a criterion, then defend what you're willing to sacrifice for it.

Win

Make the call falsifiable

State the metric, the threshold, and the timeline that proves you right or wrong.
