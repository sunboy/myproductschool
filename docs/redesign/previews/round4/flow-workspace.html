Problem Brief

Scenario

Design the cart backend for a food delivery marketplace connecting riders, restaurants, and drivers, where items can go out of stock mid-order and prices change in real time across thousands of concurrent carts.

Primary Goal

Keep every active cart consistent with live restaurant inventory and pricing while checkout stays fast under peak concurrent load.

Key Requirements

  • Reflect live item availability per restaurant
  • Handle price and menu changes mid-cart
  • Support concurrent edits across devices
  • Low-latency checkout (< 2s)
  • Scalable across peak dinner-hour load

Constraints

  • Cost effective at marketplace scale
  • Consistent across driver and rider apps
  • Data integrity during partial failures
