Acquisition

Where do they come from, and why now?

Nobody signs up for Netflix because of Netflix. They sign up because a specific show crossed into the cultural bloodstream and a friend, a group chat, or a trending tab made the show impossible to ignore. Acquisition here is not a funnel built on ads. It is a funnel built on borrowed attention, and the entire machine is tuned to convert that borrowed attention before it decays.

At 283M global subscribers across 190+ countries, the acquisition math stopped being about first-time internet users years ago. Blended CAC sits around $15, low enough that a single strong original can pay for itself in weeks, but that number hides an uneven reality. In mature markets the real cost is retention-adjacent: winning back a lapsed household costs more than winning a new one, so the acquisition team spends as much energy on win-back flows as on new sign-up. In emerging markets, mobile-only plans and regional originals do the acquisition work that a global ad budget cannot.

The trust question sits underneath all of it. A stranger has to hand over a card number, or in some markets a mobile wallet, before watching a single minute. Free trials used to absorb that risk. Removing them in most markets was a bet that word-of-mouth and a recognizable title card now carry more trust than a 30-day guarantee ever did.

War Room

3 perspectives
PM

The acquisition metric that matters isn't sign-ups, it's sign-ups that survive the first watch session. A trial that converts on the wrong show churns in month two.

Eng

Recommendation cold-start has to work in the first 90 seconds after signup, before any watch history exists. That's a harder ranking problem than personalizing for an existing account.

Data

CAC blended at $15 masks a 4x spread across markets. Aggregating it into one number hides where the growth budget is actually working.

Hatch says

The acquisition question here is really a trust question. Keep that lens for Activation: the trust a viewer hands over at sign-up is what gets spent during the first watch session.
